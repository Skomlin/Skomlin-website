The journal of the Francoise Krasinksa begins with her as a young girl living comfortably with her parents in the court of Maleszow in southern Poland. She is happy and innocent and nurses a crush on Prince Charles pretender to the Polish throne. Francoise was related to nearly all the noblest families in Poland. Eminently beautiful intelligent and witty her escapades and her fate set tongues wagging in every court in Europe.

Klementyna Tanska HoffmanAuthor
Klementyna Tanska Hoffmanowa (1798-1845) was a Polish writer and educator, among the first women in Poland to earn her living by the pen. Born in Warsaw, she wrote widely for young readers and on the moral education of women; The Journal of Countess Francoise Krasinska (1825), an imagined diary of an eighteenth-century Polish noblewoman, is her best-known work and an early example of the historical-diary novel.
