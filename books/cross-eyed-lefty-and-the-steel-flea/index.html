Cross-eyed Lefty and the Steel Flea

Nikolai Leskov (Author) · Isabel Hapgood (Translator)

ISBN 9780648182658

Format Perfect BoundPages 56USD $12.95 · GBP £9.95 · EUR €12.95 · AUD A$15.95Category FICTION / General

Tsar Alexander I of Russia while visiting England with his servant Platov is shown a number of impressive modern inventions. Platov insists that things in Russia are much better but then they are shown a mechanical flea as small as a crumb with a key to wind it up that can only be seen through a microscope. Nicolas orders Platov to find a Russian to beat the English at this game and Platov travels to Tula to find a gunsmith to do the job.

This novella is a gem of Russian literature. It is one of Leskov’s finest works and marks him as ingenious storyteller and stylistic virtuoso.

Contributors

Nikolai LeskovAuthor
Nikolai Leskov (1831-1895) was a Russian novelist, short-story writer and journalist, admired by Chekhov and Gorky for his mastery of vernacular Russian and his vivid portraits of provincial and clerical life. Born in Gorokhovo, Oryol Province, he drew on wide travels across Russia; his tale Lady Macbeth of the Mtsensk District later inspired Shostakovich's opera. Skomlin also publishes Leskov's Lady Macbeth of the Mzinsk District.
