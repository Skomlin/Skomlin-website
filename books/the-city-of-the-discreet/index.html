The City of the Discreet is set in the traditional Andalusia of the 19th century. This gentle parody with its vast gallery of characters demonstrates Baroja’s powers of description and his subtle humour. The description of his Cordova and its environs are an invaluable guide to a recent but now lost city.

Contributors

Pío BarojaAuthor
Pio Baroja (1872-1956) was a Basque-Spanish novelist, a central figure of Spain's 'Generation of '98' whom Hemingway openly admired. Born in San Sebastian, he wrote spare, restless fiction of Spanish life across dozens of novels. The City of the Discreet is set among the schemers and dreamers of Cordoba.
